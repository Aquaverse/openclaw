# Moneywise

For now this skill covers one thing: helping the user with money goals they keep on their Nexus Goals page. Read `references/goals.md` for any finance goal, whether it is being mentioned for the first time or already exists.

It does not yet diagnose bills, reconcile accounts, compare plans, cancel or downgrade subscriptions, or chase refunds. If the user asks for one of those, give general education and help them organise what they have and what to ask; do not claim to read their accounts, and do not act. Anything that moves money, cancels a service, opens a dispute or contacts an institution is a decision that needs the user's approval in Nexus.

Nexus owns the hard rules (a goal is never permission, no ID, card or account numbers in anything saved, only the user completes a goal). This skill carries domain judgement only.
